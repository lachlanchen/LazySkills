# Paired Free And Paid Series

Owner preference, 2026-10-13. Apply when planning a new LALACHAN sticker series.
This changes the brief, not the established reference, animation or GIF pipeline.

## Scope

- One free pack of 16 useful core replies and one paid pack of 16 additional
  animations. Both are complete, polished packs, related by theme and palette.
- All 32 animations are distinct, also checked against selected earlier packs.
  Shared characters and recurring trigger words are welcome; reused animation
  with different lettering is not a new sticker. Language editions of the same
  sticker retain their shared provenance.
- Each pack has distinct cover, icon, banner, appreciation and thank-you artwork.
  Keep appreciation enabled where available, independently of paid access.
- Preserve prior editions, prices and approvals. Respect explicit later scope
  changes. Verify platform support before upload; a planning preference is not
  an instruction to submit existing drafts or spend generation credits now.

## Choose Replies People Would Send

Start with a familiar chat situation, the reply someone would actually send,
and a readable action. Cute exaggeration helps when the situation is instantly
recognizable. A small surprise or affectionate reaction can make it memorable;
usefulness does not require an elaborate story.

The owner's example pool is flexible, not a compulsory 32-item list:

| Direction | Example lettering / situations |
| --- | --- |
| Free core | 嗨、早安、晚安、谢谢、不客气、客气了、对不起、拜托了、委屈、汗、开心、哭哭、好饿、大吃、运动、加油 |
| Paid expansion | 努力、恭喜、好棒、厉害、赞、笑掉大牙、掏鼻孔、生气、蓝色冷汗、吓一跳、炸毛、我在、带走、一起睡觉、抱抱、摸摸头 |
| Other useful moments | 玩手机、拍照、坐着后仰找拍摄角度、单手提起伙伴带走 |

Choose short natural captions suited to the action. For instance, an eating
scene might work better as 开饭啦 than 大吃; a workout can say 动起来. Keep
platform search meanings clear, with the existing Mandarin-trigger convention
for Cantonese/phonetic-Japanese lettering. These are choices, not substitutions
that must be applied to every pack.

Learn from Yier/Bubu's everyday communication, affectionate reactions and clear
silhouettes while preserving LALACHAN's own identities, poses and artwork.

## Balance The Cast

Review the existing catalog as well as the proposed pair. Aya need not deliver
all the warm replies. Give Lala more 谢谢、不客气、恭喜恭喜、加油 and 努力.
Let Sasa and Zhuangzi be thankful, sleepy, pleased or affectionate too, rather
than assigning them only comedy or work. Preserve Aya's brave, playful side.

Rotate common greetings, especially 早安 and 晚安, so all four have opportunities
over successive series. This is coverage over the catalog, not a demand to use
eight slots on morning/night in every pair. A character-led theme can favor its
lead; avoid a numerical quota that weakens the scene.

Use a simple planning table: pack/free-or-paid, trigger, visible text, lead,
partner if any, action/reaction, intended chat use, and native-animation source
or new-generation status. Review gaps before rendering. Pair interactions count
as scenes, not a reason to crowd every composition with all four buddies.

## Natural Acting And Prompts

Use the approved character reference and a concise positive action description:
setup, purposeful movement, reaction, then a relaxed finish. Describe visible
joint movement and weight where helpful. Keep a readable pause for sending and
a smooth loop or loop transition. The existing small outlined animated text and
edge layout remain the default.

Possible concepts, not a fixed shot list:

- **带走:** 庄子一只手提起啦啦侠背带的提手，啦啦侠脚尖离地，愣一下后笑着晃晃脚。庄子侧身迈出两小步，啦啦侠朝镜头挥手。
- **玩手机:** 飒飒君窝在小椅子里，拇指轻轻滑动手机，看到有趣的消息后肩膀笑得一抖，抬头露出得意的笑。
- **拍照:** 阿芽酱坐在凳子上，双手举起手机，背轻轻后仰找角度，眯起一只眼，按下快门后满意地点头。
- **谢谢:** 啦啦侠接过小礼物，眼睛一亮，抱到胸前，微微鞠躬，再抬头笑着挥挥手。

The carrying gesture is playful, with stable grip and a comfortable partner;
choose a different buddy or action to suit the theme. Text is composited later
using the accepted workflow, so the image/video prompt can focus on acting.

Review the whole action at chat size: clear emotion, believable limbs/contact,
consistent palette, smooth movement and legible text. A rigid full-body rotation
or floating phone does not communicate the intended action. Use the existing
bounded local correction rules and preserve every alternative in the gallery.
Do not rerender already accepted packs merely to rebalance the cast.
